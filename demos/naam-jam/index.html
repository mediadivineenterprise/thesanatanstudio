Tour dates

Each jam is capped at 120 people so everyone can hear themselves sing.

One evening, four parts

Show up a little early. The light at 5:30 is the whole point.

5:00Chai and settle in

Cushions, masala chai, meet the people around you.

5:30Sunset kirtan

Slow call-and-response as the sky changes colour.

6:30Open jam

Musicians from the crowd join in. Tempo goes up.

7:30Silence and prasad

Five quiet minutes, then sweets and goodbyes.
