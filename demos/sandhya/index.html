Questions

Can I bring children and elders?

Yes. Chairs are reserved for Seva and Yajman passes, and there is lift access to the hall.

Is there parking?

Two-wheeler parking inside the premises and car parking on the lane behind the hall.

Can I offer seva without attending?

Yes. Choose a Seva Pass and mention it in the WhatsApp chat. Your name is still read at aarti.

Do you organise Sandhyas for housing societies or temples?

Yes. Send us the date and expected crowd on WhatsApp and we share options within a day.
