Quick questions

Is it actually a party or a satsang?

Both. It's a live concert where every song is a bhajan. People sing, dance and leave feeling light.

Alcohol?

None. It's a sober night. The mocktail and chai bar runs all evening.

Can I bring my parents?

Yes, and they usually end up dancing harder than you. Age 12+ only.

What should I wear?

Anything comfortable. Most people go kurta with sneakers. Shoes stay on, it's a club floor.

Refunds?

No refunds, but you can transfer your pass to a friend up to 24 hours before doors.
