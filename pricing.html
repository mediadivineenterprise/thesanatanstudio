FAQ

Common Questions

Is the ad budget included in the pricing?

No. The ad budget (minimum ₹5,000/month recommended) is paid by you directly to Meta/Instagram. We handle the setup, targeting, and creative — we don't markup ad spend.

Do you travel to events or help with venue booking?

No. We are a digital agency — we work online only. We handle all promotion, ticketing, and digital setup. Venue booking, artist coordination, and event day management is handled by you.

What videos do you need from us for Reels?

You record any footage from the event — even phone camera is fine. Send it to us on WhatsApp and we edit it into a professional Reel with music, captions, and effects.

How long does the setup take?

Typically 10–14 working days for the initial setup. After that, monthly deliverables are on a fixed schedule agreed at the start.

What is a Sponsorship Deck?

A professional PDF (8–10 pages) to show local businesses why they should sponsor your event. Includes your event stats, audience details, and sponsorship benefits. Add-on at ₹5,000.

Can I start with Starter and upgrade later?

Yes! Many clients start with Starter, then upgrade to Event Organiser once their first event needs ticketing and regular posting. You only pay the difference in setup.

Is there a contract or lock-in period?

Minimum 3-month commitment for monthly plans (Event Organiser and Growth). This ensures you see real results from consistent content and community building.

What's the difference between Event Organiser and Event Launch?

Event Organiser is an ongoing monthly retainer for groups running events regularly. Event Launch is a one-time, all-in push for a single big event — with no monthly commitment after.
